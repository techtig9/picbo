Privacy Policy

We collect the information you provide at signup (name, email, country), the prompts and reference files you submit for generation, and basic usage data (credits spent, features used) to operate and improve the product.

Prompts and uploaded reference images are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate your results — this is the only third-party AI processor we use. Payment details are handled entirely by Paddle, our Merchant of Record; we never see or store your card number.
