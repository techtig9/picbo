Questions

Frequently asked.

How does the credit system work?

Every generation costs a fixed number of credits based on what you're making — 40 for a simple photo up to 500 for a 15-second animated ad. Free accounts get 500 credits a month; paid plans get a much larger monthly pool, plus top-up packs if you need more.

Is the "animated ad" real AI video?

No, and we want to be upfront about that. It's a 15-second, 1080p slideshow — Gemini-generated photos assembled with pan, zoom and transitions, plus optional captions and music. It's simpler, faster and cheaper than generative video, and it's exactly what most ad placements actually need.

How do I pay, and can I cancel anytime?

Checkout runs through Paddle, so your card is billed securely with tax handled automatically wherever you are. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel anytime from Billing — changes are prorated.

Can I use generations commercially?

Yes — all paid plans include a commercial license. Free-plan exports carry a watermark and are for personal use.

What if I run out of credits mid-month?

Buy a top-up pack anytime, or on Business and Agency plans, turn on pay-as-you-go overage so you're never blocked mid-project. Ask Lumi for a recommendation based on your usage.
