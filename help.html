Popular questions

Quick answers.

Why did my generation use more credits than expected?

Cost depends on what you're making — a "simple" photo (single subject, no complex scene) is 40 credits, while multi-element or edited photos are 80. Ad creatives, photoshoots and animated ads scale up from there, up to 500 for a 15-second animated ad.

How does billing work?

Checkout is handled by Paddle, our Merchant of Record — your card is charged securely and tax is calculated automatically. You'll get an email receipt the moment your plan activates.

My free credits haven't refreshed yet

Free-plan credits refresh monthly. If it's been longer than a month since your last reset, log out and back in, or contact support.

Can I get a refund?

Reach out to Techtig9@gmail.com within 7 days of payment with your transaction ID — refund requests are reviewed case by case.
